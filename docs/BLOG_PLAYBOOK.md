# Blog Playbook for AME Bazaar

This document is the permanent blogging handbook for AME Bazaar. It is meant for future content creators, marketing helpers, and AI assistants.

## 1. Blog Writing SOP

### Purpose of every blog
Every blog should help a real customer or a real searcher.
A good blog should:
- answer a real question
- help someone make a better buying decision
- improve trust in the brand
- support SEO and AI discoverability
- support sales or store visits

### Blog writing rules
- Write in clear, simple, helpful language.
- Keep sentences short and easy to read.
- Focus on useful information over hype.
- Use real store facts and practical advice.
- Speak like a helpful store expert, not a robot.
- Avoid fake claims, exaggerated promises, or vague marketing words.

### Blog structure
Every blog should follow this structure:
1. Strong title
2. Short intro
3. Clear subheadings
4. Helpful body content
5. Practical conclusion
6. CTA to store, product, or contact page

### Recommended blog length
- Short blog: 600 to 900 words
- Standard blog: 900 to 1,500 words
- Deep guide: 1,500+ words

## 2. SEO Checklist
Before publishing, check all of the following:
- Title is clear and includes a useful keyword
- First paragraph explains the topic quickly
- Heading structure is clean and logical
- Content answers the search intent
- Content is original and useful
- Internal links are included
- Product links are included where useful
- Meta title and description are clear
- URL is short and readable
- Images are optimized and relevant
- Content is easy to read on mobile

## 3. GEO Checklist
GEO means Generative Engine Optimization. This is about helping AI tools find and understand the content.

Before publishing, confirm:
- The article is factual and useful
- The article answers a clear question
- The article uses clear headings
- The article includes FAQ-style information where useful
- The article is structured in a natural way
- The article matches the business’s real services and products
- The article can be understood by both humans and AI systems

## 4. AI Discoverability Checklist
To improve AI discoverability:
- Write clearly and directly
- Use focused topics and natural language
- Provide practical answers
- Include synonyms and related phrases naturally
- Add FAQ sections when helpful
- Add internal links to relevant authority pages and products
- Keep facts consistent with the rest of the website
- Avoid generic filler content

## 5. Internal Linking Rules
Internal links are important for both SEO and AI understanding.

Rules:
- Link to relevant product pages when helpful
- Link to relevant category pages
- Link to related blog posts
- Link to authority pages and guide pages
- Use natural anchor text
- Do not force links into awkward places
- Link to pages that truly support the article

### Good internal link examples
- “Learn more about our tailoring services.”
- “Explore our women’s wear collection.”
- “Read our fabric guide for more details.”

## 6. Image Rules
Images should support the article and improve trust.

Rules:
- Use real and relevant images where possible
- Use clear, bright, and high-quality images
- Keep visuals professional
- Avoid cluttered or irrelevant images
- Make sure images support the topic
- Use images that fit the AME Bazaar brand

## 7. Alt Text Rules
Alt text should describe the image in a useful and natural way.

Rules:
- Keep it short and descriptive
- Mention the key subject clearly
- Include relevant local or product context when helpful
- Do not stuff keywords unnaturally
- Do not leave alt text empty if the image is meaningful

### Example alt text
- “Women’s cotton kurti display at AME Bazaar store”
- “Tailoring service table with fabric samples”
- “Customer trying on a fitted shirt in the store”

## 8. Schema Rules
Use schema only when it is appropriate and helpful.

Recommended schema use:
- FAQ schema for question-and-answer content
- Article schema for blog posts
- Product schema when a product is the main focus
- Local business schema where relevant

Rules:
- Keep schema factual
- Do not add misleading schema
- Make sure the content matches the schema
- Prefer simple and accurate structured data

## 9. FAQ Generation
Every important article should include a FAQ section when it helps the reader.

FAQ topics can include:
- common questions about fit
- common questions about fabric care
- common questions about seasonal clothing
- common questions about tailoring
- common questions about shopping in-store

### FAQ writing rules
- Keep answers short and useful
- Use common language
- Answer the question directly
- Avoid unnecessary fluff

## 10. Product Linking
Articles should connect readers to relevant products when it makes sense.

Rules:
- Link to the most relevant products
- Use helpful anchor text
- Link only to products that truly match the article topic
- Avoid random product linking
- Prefer product links that support buying decisions

### Example
If writing about summer cotton wear, link to cotton clothing products or collections.

## 11. CTA Strategy
Every blog should end with a clear call to action.

CTA options:
- Visit the store
- Contact us for tailoring
- Browse the collection
- Read another related article
- Shop the featured product range
- Ask for help via WhatsApp

CTA rules:
- Keep it simple
- Make it natural
- Not too pushy
- Relevant to the article

## 12. Publishing Workflow
Use this workflow for every blog post:
1. Choose a topic
2. Research the topic and customer intent
3. Create the draft
4. Review for clarity and usefulness
5. Add headings, FAQs, and internal links
6. Add images and alt text
7. Review SEO and GEO checklist
8. Add CTA
9. Preview the post
10. Publish
11. Share if needed

## 13. Monthly Content Calendar
Each month, publish a balanced mix of content:
- 1 helpful buying guide
- 1 educational article about fabrics or care
- 1 local SEO blog
- 1 trend or style article
- 1 product-focused article
- 1 FAQ or customer education article

### Example monthly mix
- Week 1: Fabric guide
- Week 2: Seasonal clothing guide
- Week 3: Local shopping guide
- Week 4: Product education or tailoring article

- festive family outfit ideas
- wedding shopping advice
- gifting ideas
- matching outfit suggestions

Festival blogs should connect to products and store services where possible.

## 16. Local SEO Blogging Strategy
Local blogs should focus on nearby customers and real search intent.

Topics can include:
- best clothing store in Kirari
- tailoring near me
- where to buy festive wear locally
- family clothing shopping in Delhi
- clothing shopping advice near your area

Local blogs should mention the store, location, and community relevance naturally.

## 17. Clothing Buying Guides
Buying guides should help customers make better choices.

Topics can include:
- how to choose the right shirt fit
- how to pick the right fabric
- how to buy clothing for weddings
- how to choose clothing for kids
- how to choose clothing for different seasons

These guides should be practical and easy to follow.

## 18. Fashion Trends
Trend content should be helpful, not superficial.

Use trend content to:
- highlight what customers are asking about
- connect trends to real product options
- explain how to wear or style a trend practically

Avoid writing trend articles that are shallow or purely decorative.

## 19. Fabric Education
Fabric content is important because it builds trust and helps customers choose better.

Topics can include:
- cotton vs rayon
- breathable fabrics for summer
- how GSM works
- fabric care basics
- why fabric matters for comfort and durability

## 20. Customer Education
Customer education content should make shopping easier.

Topics can include:
- how tailoring works
- how to care for garments
- how to choose sizes
- how to shop for family wear
- how to prepare for festive shopping

## 21. Reusable Blog Templates

### Template A: Helpful Guide Blog
Title:
- [Clear topic + benefit]

Intro:
- 2 to 3 short paragraphs explaining the problem and why the article matters.

Sections:
- Why this matters
- What to look for
- How to choose
- Common mistakes
- Final advice

CTA:
- Visit the store or browse related products.

### Template B: FAQ Blog
Title:
- [Common customer question]

Intro:
- Brief answer to the question.

Sections:
- What people often ask
- Best answer
- When to choose this option
- Related products or services

FAQ:
- Add 4 to 6 common questions.

CTA:
- Contact us or visit the store.

### Template C: Local SEO Blog
Title:
- [Local need + location + service]

Intro:
- Mention the local context clearly.

Sections:
- Why local shoppers care
- What AME Bazaar offers
- What customers should know
- Where to visit or contact

CTA:
- Visit the store or contact us.

### Template D: Product Education Blog
Title:
- [Product type + benefit]

Intro:
- Explain what the product is and why it matters.

Sections:
- Key features
- Fit and usage
- Care and maintenance
- Who should buy it

CTA:
- Shop the related product range.

## 22. Content Quality Rules
- Do not publish thin or repetitive content
- Do not publish content that is not useful
- Do not copy from other sites
- Keep the tone helpful and human
- Make every article feel like it belongs to AME Bazaar

## 23. Final Publishing Checklist
Before publishing, verify:
- Title is strong and clear
- Intro is useful
- Headings are logical
- Content is complete
- Internal links are added
- Products are linked where relevant
- Images are present and optimized
- Alt text is written
- CTA is included
- Content is ready for SEO and AI discovery
