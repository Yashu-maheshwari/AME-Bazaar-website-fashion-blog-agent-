# Admin Settings & Operations Guide

This guide walks non-technical store managers through configuring maps, review ratings, and sharing QR codes.

## Step-by-Step Dashboard Setup

1. **Accessing the settings**:
   - Log into the WordPress dashboard.
   - Navigate to the **AME Store** menu on the left sidebar.
   - Select **Google Business Profile**.

2. **Core Configured Modules**:
   - **Business Profile**: Change Name, Categories, Description, and attributes.
   - **Hours & Exceptions**: Update opening hours and specify holiday exemptions.
   - **Google Review Settings**: Add direct review request link, overall rating, and counts.
   - **Maps Embed details**: Paste standard iframe embed URLs, latitude, longitude, and CIDs.

3. **Generating printable QR materials**:
   - Click the **Reviews & Authority** submenu tab.
   - Download stand cards, posters, and insertion cards. Print them on paper or counter stand boards.
