# AI Memory for AME Bazaar

This document is the permanent memory file for every future AI working on the AME Bazaar project.
It must be read before making any change.

## 1. Business Identity
- AME Bazaar is a local clothing and fashion retail business.
- The business is rooted in family-style retail service, trust, and local customer relationships.
- The brand is not a generic online store. It is a real local business with physical presence and strong community relevance.
- The business should always feel warm, trustworthy, premium, and practical.

## 2. Company Mission
- Help local customers find quality fashion, reliable tailoring, and a trustworthy shopping experience.
- Make the store easy to discover online and easy to trust.
- Turn the website into a strong sales and reputation platform for the physical store.

## 3. Long-term Vision
- Build AME Bazaar into a recognized local fashion brand with strong online visibility.
- Become known not just as a shop, but as a trusted source for fashion guidance, tailoring, local shopping, and retail expertise.
- Grow from a local store into a stronger digital retail presence over time.

## 4. Target Customers
- Local customers in and around Kirari, Delhi.
- Families shopping for clothing across age groups.
- Customers looking for quality fashion at reasonable prices.
- Customers who value tailoring, fit, and personal service.
- Customers searching for fashion advice and local store options.

## 5. Brand Voice
- Friendly
- Professional
- Trustworthy
- Helpful
- Local and human
- Not overly formal
- Not robotic
- Not overly salesy

## 6. Writing Style
- Clear and simple
- Useful and informative
- Factual and grounded
- Calm and confident
- Customer-focused
- Avoid fake hype or exaggerated claims
- Avoid empty marketing phrases

## 7. Website Goals
- Present the business clearly and professionally
- Make products easy to understand and browse
- Encourage visits to the physical store
- Encourage contact and inquiries
- Support local discovery and online conversions
- Build long-term trust with users and search engines

## 8. SEO Goals
- Improve rankings for relevant local and product searches
- Keep business information consistent across the site
- Publish useful content that matches real customer questions
- Strengthen product pages, category pages, and guide pages
- Build topical authority around fashion, tailoring, and local retail

## 9. GEO (AI Discoverability) Goals
- Make the business easy for AI systems to understand
- Create content that is structured, factual, and helpful
- Support AI tools with clear FAQs, authority pages, local business context, and schema markup
- Help Google, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity discover and reference the brand accurately

## 10. Google Business Profile Facts
- The business is a local retail clothing store
- The business is associated with AME Bazaar
- Store location is linked to Kirari, Delhi
- The store is known for fashion, apparel, tailoring, and local customer service
- Business details should remain consistent with the website information
- Google Business Profile details should be treated as a source of truth for local identity

## 11. Store Information
- The business is a physical retail store
- The store is based in Kirari, Delhi
- The store is connected to local fashion retail and tailoring services
- The website should reinforce this local presence rather than feel purely digital

## 12. Brand Colors
- The site uses a premium, refined, retail-friendly visual style
- Colors should feel trustworthy, elegant, and grounded in a fashion retail identity
- Avoid overly bright or chaotic color use
- Preserve the established visual tone of the theme

## 13. Typography
- Typography should remain clean, readable, and premium
- Font choices should support clarity and fashion retail presentation
- Do not introduce visual styles that make the site feel noisy or unprofessional

## 14. Homepage Philosophy
- The homepage is the main business entry point
- It should feel welcoming, trustworthy, and useful
- It should guide visitors toward products, store information, trust, and contact
- It should not feel like a generic blog or an unrelated landing page

## 15. Homepage Sections
The homepage should remain structured around the existing modular sections, including:
- hero section
- premium local trust section (8 dynamic trust cards linked to location, whatsapp, and reviews, plus an AI authority badge row)
- categories (with Boys Wear and Girls Wear split)
- featured collections
- tailoring service
- why choose us
- reviews
- visit store section
- about business section
- blog preview
- Instagram gallery
- WhatsApp CTA

These sections should stay in place unless a change is explicitly justified and safe.

## 16. WooCommerce Philosophy
- WooCommerce is a tool for selling products, not just displaying catalog items
- Product pages should feel useful, clear, and retail-oriented
- Product cards should support browsing and conversion
- The store should retain local retail cues such as tailoring, store pickup, and personal service
- Do not replace the storefront experience with a generic commerce layout

## 17. Blog Strategy
- Blogs should support trust, helpfulness, and discoverability
- Blogs should answer real customer questions
- Blogs should be practical, local, and relevant
- Blogs should support SEO and AI discoverability
- Blog topics should be useful to shoppers and searchers

## 18. Product Strategy
- Products should be presented clearly and professionally
- Descriptions should be helpful and specific
- Product images should be high quality
- Product metadata should be accurate
- Product pages should support both conversion and search visibility

## 19. Image Strategy
- Images must be meaningful, relevant, and high quality
- Images should support the brand and product story
- Avoid cluttered or low-quality visuals
- Use real store and product images when possible
- Every image should contribute to trust or clarity

## 20. Media Library Rules
- Media should be uploaded through the WordPress Media Library
- Image assignments should be managed carefully
- Do not hardcode image URLs when a media mapping approach is available
- Keep alt text, captions, and descriptions meaningful
- Preserve the media workflow already used by the theme

## 21. Local SEO Strategy
- Emphasize local relevance
- Keep location details consistent everywhere
- Use local terms naturally in content and metadata
- Support discovery for nearby customers and local search intent
- Reinforce the physical store experience

## 22. Schema Strategy
- Schema should be used to clarify business, local entity, FAQ, and product information
- Keep structured data factual and consistent
- Do not add irrelevant or conflicting schema
- Preserve the existing semantic layout and structured content approach

## 23. AI Fashion Advisor Strategy
- The AI Fashion Advisor should remain helpful, practical, and grounded in the store’s actual business context
- It should support customer questions about style, fit, tailoring, fabrics, care, and purchasing advice
- It should not feel generic or detached from the business
- It should align with the brand’s retail identity and local store experience

## 24. Performance Rules
- Keep the site fast and lightweight
- Avoid unnecessary scripts and heavy features
- Optimize images and page output
- Preserve a smooth experience for mobile and desktop users
- Do not add features that significantly hurt performance without strong reason

## 25. Security Rules
- Never trust user input blindly
- Escape output and sanitize input
- Follow WordPress security best practices
- Respect permissions and admin restrictions
- Do not introduce unsafe code or insecure external behavior

## 26. Coding Rules
- Use the existing naming conventions and structure
- Prefer small, scoped changes over broad rewrites
- Keep the code aligned with the current theme architecture
- Use existing helpers and hooks where possible
- Preserve readability and maintainability
- Do not introduce unnecessary dependencies
- Do not change code without understanding the existing system first

## 27. Git Workflow
- Do not make changes casually
- Understand the existing implementation before editing
- Keep changes small and reviewable
- Do not commit or push unless explicitly requested
- Verify changes before considering them complete

## 28. Deployment Workflow
- Treat the local workspace as the source of truth until deployment is requested
- Validate changes before deployment
- Preserve content, media mappings, templates, and business settings during deployment
- Avoid deploying without confirming the storefront still works as expected

## 29. Things That Should NEVER Change
The following should be treated as highly sensitive and should not be changed casually:
- the core business identity
- the brand voice and tone
- the store location and business facts
- the core homepage structure
- the main business pages such as About, Contact, FAQ, Shop
- the AI Fashion Advisor experience
- the core WooCommerce experience
- the main SEO and schema architecture
- the primary media mapping workflow
- the established theme bootstrap and module structure

## 30. Future Roadmap
### 3 Months
- strengthen local SEO
- improve content depth and quality
- improve product presentation
- expand useful blog and guide content
- improve local trust signals

### 1 Year
- deepen AI discoverability and structured content coverage
- improve product storytelling and merchandising
- strengthen authority pages and FAQ coverage
- improve site performance and conversion flow

### 5 Years
- build a stronger national or regional digital fashion presence
- expand the brand’s authority and recognition
- deepen AI visibility and content relevance
- evolve the site into a long-term retail and content platform

## Always Remember
- AME Bazaar is a real local retail business, not a generic theme demo.
- The website must feel trustworthy, helpful, and local.
- Every change should support business growth, customer trust, and discoverability.
- SEO, GEO, and local relevance are core goals.
- Do not damage the homepage, product experience, or brand identity.
- Preserve the existing architecture unless a change is clearly justified.
- Before writing code, always remember the business purpose behind the website.
