# Business Operating Manual for AME Bazaar

This manual is for the business owner. It is written in very simple language. No coding knowledge is needed.

## 1. Complete Website Overview
Your website is your online shop and business front. It helps customers:
- see your products
- learn about your store
- contact you
- find your location
- trust your business
- buy from you online or visit your shop

The website is made to support both:
- local customers in Delhi and nearby areas
- online visitors who may discover your business through Google or AI tools

The main goals of the website are to:
- show your brand clearly
- present products in a clean way
- make it easy to contact you
- improve your search visibility
- help people find the store easily

## 2. Which Pages Exist and Why
The website includes several important pages.

### Home Page
This is the main page people see first. It shows:
- your brand
- promotions or featured collections
- store information
- important product categories
- trust signals
- links to other pages

### About Page
This page explains who you are, what your business does, and why customers should trust you.

### Contact Page
This page gives people your contact details and location.

### FAQ Page
This page answers common questions, such as:
- store location
- tailoring services
- delivery questions
- product care
- general shopping questions

### AI Fashion Advisor Page
This page helps visitors ask questions about clothing, fitting, fabric, and shopping advice.

### Shop Page
This is where your products are shown.

### Blog / Articles Page
This page helps your business appear more useful and more trustworthy online.

### Authority / Guide Pages
These pages help Google and AI tools understand your business better. They cover topics such as:
- best clothing store in your area
- tailoring near you
- fabric guides
- fitting guides
- wedding shopping

## 3. How to Update Products
To update products:
1. Log in to your WordPress dashboard.
2. Open Products.
3. Click the product you want to edit.
4. Update the product title, description, price, images, and stock.
5. Save or publish the product.

Important things to check:
- product title should be clear
- description should be simple and helpful
- images should be bright and clear
- price should be correct
- stock should be updated

If a product is sold out, mark it clearly.

## 4. How to Upload Product Images
To upload product images:
1. Open the product page.
2. Find the image section.
3. Click Add Product Image.
4. Choose an image from your computer.
5. Upload it.
6. Save the product.

Best image tips:
- use clear and bright photos
- avoid blurry pictures
- show the product clearly
- use real photos when possible
- keep the background simple

If you have multiple images, add more than one so customers can see the product from different angles.

## 5. How to Replace Homepage Banners
Homepage banners are the large images or sliders shown at the top of the home page.

To replace them:
1. Go to the website admin area.
2. Open the media or homepage settings section.
3. Choose the new image.
4. Save the change.

Tips:
- use clean, high-quality images
- make sure the image fits the brand
- do not use random or low-quality pictures
- use images that show your store, products, or style

## 6. How to Change Store Photos
Store photos are important because they make your business feel real and trusted.

To change store photos:
1. Go to the media section.
2. Upload the new image.
3. Open the place where the image is assigned.
4. Select the new image.
5. Save changes.

Good store photos include:
- front of store
- inside view
- fitting or tailoring area
- happy customers or staff if appropriate
- clean and professional spaces

## 7. How to Update Business Information
Business information should always be accurate.

Update these details when needed:
- business name
- phone number
- WhatsApp number
- email address
- store address
- opening hours
- map link
- social media links

To update this:
1. Go to the business settings area.
2. Edit the information.
3. Save the changes.

Always double-check the information before saving.

## 8. How to Update Logo
To update the logo:
1. Go to the media area.
2. Upload the new logo file.
3. Open the logo settings section.
4. Select the new logo.
5. Save it.

Important rules:
- the logo should be clear
- it should match your brand style
- it should look good on light and dark backgrounds
- it should be easy to read

## 9. How to Write Blogs
Blogs help your website look fresh and useful.

A simple blog should:
- answer a customer question
- share helpful fashion advice
- explain a product or style
- explain your services
- talk about local shopping needs

Good blog topics include:
- how to choose the right fabric
- how to shop for wedding wear
- how to care for clothes
- how to choose clothing for different seasons
- why customers should visit your store

Keep the writing:
- simple
- helpful
- clear
- honest
- natural

## 10. How to Publish Blogs
To publish a blog:
1. Go to Posts in the dashboard.
2. Click Add New.
3. Write the title and content.
4. Add images if needed.
5. Add category or tags if available.
6. Preview the article.
7. Click Publish.

Before publishing, check:
- spelling
- grammar
- image quality
- title clarity
- links to your products or store pages

## 11. How to Improve Google Rankings
Google rankings improve when your website is helpful, clear, and updated.

Do these regularly:
- update products regularly
- add new blog content
- use clear titles and headings
- use real images
- keep business information correct
- add location information
- make sure the website loads quickly
- make sure pages are easy to read

Important tips:
- use words your customers actually search for
- mention your location clearly
- mention your products clearly
- keep the website fresh

## 12. How to Improve AI Rankings (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity)
AI tools look for clear, factual, and useful content.

To improve your chance of being mentioned:
- publish helpful content regularly
- use clear headings and answers
- write content that answers real customer questions
- keep information accurate and consistent
- add FAQ pages and guide pages
- make sure your business details are the same everywhere
- publish content around your real products and services

AI tools prefer content that is:
- trustworthy
- specific
- useful
- clearly written
- local and factual

## 13. What Should Never Be Deleted
These items should not be deleted unless you are fully sure:
- homepage core structure
- business contact information
- product pages
- important store pages like About, Contact, FAQ, Shop
- logo and main brand images
- key blog pages and guide pages
- important SEO and business pages

If you are unsure, do not delete anything. Ask for help first.

## 14. Monthly Maintenance Checklist
Every month:
- review product prices and stock
- update old or missing product images
- check business phone, address, and hours
- review homepage banners and store photos
- publish at least one helpful blog or article
- check that all links work
- review Google Business information
- review contact forms and WhatsApp links
- check the website on mobile phone

## 15. Weekly Maintenance Checklist
Every week:
- add or update a few products if needed
- answer customer questions quickly
- review new orders or inquiries
- check if any page looks broken
- check if any images are missing
- review blog content ideas
- confirm store information remains correct

## 16. Daily Maintenance Checklist
Every day:
- check if new orders or customer messages came in
- make sure the website is working normally
- review any urgent product or stock updates
- check if anything on the homepage looks wrong
- keep business contact details current

## 17. Backup Process
Backups protect your website if something goes wrong.

You should:
- use your hosting provider backup system if available
- keep a copy of important images and documents
- save business details separately
- keep a copy of new blog posts and product updates

If a backup option is available in your hosting panel, use it regularly.

## 18. How to Recover from Mistakes
If something goes wrong:
1. Stay calm.
2. Do not make many changes at once.
3. Check whether the problem is a small content issue or a bigger website issue.
4. Restore from backup if needed.
5. Contact support if the issue is technical.

Common mistakes include:
- deleting the wrong page
- uploading the wrong image
- changing the wrong product
- publishing unfinished content

If you are unsure, stop and ask for help before changing more.

Future goals may include:

## 20. FAQ for the Website Owner
### Q: Do I need to know coding to run this website?
No. You only need to learn the simple actions in this manual.

### Q: Can I change the homepage myself?
Yes, if you follow the simple steps for banners and business information.

### Q: What if I upload the wrong image?
You can replace it with a correct one. If you are unsure, ask for help.

### Q: What if I delete something by mistake?
Stop and restore from backup if possible. Do not keep changing things.

### Q: How often should I update the website?
At least weekly, and ideally daily for important business updates.

### Q: Why are blogs important?
Blogs help customers and AI tools understand your business better.

### Q: Why is good product information important?
Good product information helps customers buy and helps search engines understand your store.

### Q: Should I update the business details often?
Yes. Keep them accurate at all times.

### Q: What is the most important thing to keep updated?
Your products, business information, and store images.
