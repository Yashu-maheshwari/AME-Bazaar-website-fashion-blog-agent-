# AME Bazaar Project Roadmap

This roadmap outlines the planned growth of AME Bazaar from the current foundation to Version 5.0.
It is designed for business growth, marketing growth, technology growth, and long-term digital strength.

## Phase 1 (Completed)

### Objective
Build the core storefront foundation for AME Bazaar with local retail focus, strong branding, modular templates, and initial SEO and AI discoverability support.

### Business Impact
- Establishes a professional online presence
- Makes the business easier to trust
- Improves local discoverability
- Creates the foundation for future growth

### Technical Tasks
- Theme architecture and modular structure
- Core homepage sections
- Business information system
- Media mapping workflow
- Basic WooCommerce storefront integration
- Initial SEO and schema setup
- AI advisor and authority page structure
- Blog and content framework basics

### Priority
High

### Estimated Time
Completed

### Expected ROI
High, because it creates the base for all future growth
