## 1. Brand Identity & Copywriting Rules
- **Brand Name:** Always display as **AME Bazaar** (Not "AMEBazaar", "Ame bazaar", or "Maheshwari Bazaar").
- **Legal Entity:** Represented as **Apparel Maheshwari Enterprises** in official footers and terms.
- **Value Proposition:** Focus on "Quality, Affordability, and Trust." Do not advertise deep discounts, cheap closeouts, or bargain-bin pricing. We compete on quality and customer service.
- **Target Audience:** Family shopping (Men, Women, Kids). Copy must remain family-friendly, professional, and welcoming.

## 2. Location & Operating Hours
- **Physical Address:** Mubarakpur Road, Kirari, Delhi, India.
- **Service Area:** Kirari, Mubarakpur, Baljit Vihar, Inder Enclave, Prem Nagar, and neighboring Delhi districts.
- **Store Hours:** Operating hours must match official Google Business Profile configurations.

## 3. Product Catalog & Retail Rules
- **No Direct Online Shipping Yet:** Until WooCommerce is officially enabled, all product pages must feature a "Check Availability on WhatsApp" or "Inquire via Call" button.
- **Exchange Policy:** Exchanges are allowed within 7 days of purchase at the physical store with original receipt and tags intact. No cash refunds.
- **Pricing:** Listed prices are fixed and fair. Online prices must match in-store pricing exactly.
