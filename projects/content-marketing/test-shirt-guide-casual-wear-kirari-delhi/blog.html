What Exactly is a Benchmark Test Shirt?

A benchmark or Test Shirt is a high-quality standard casual shirt that you use as a reference point for fit, fabric comfort, and stitching quality. When you find one that fits perfectly across your shoulders and feels gentle on your skin, every future garment purchase becomes much easier to evaluate.

5 Steps to Evaluate Your Test Shirt Before Buying

Before buying any casual wear for yourself or your family, bring it through our recommended quality evaluation checklist right here at AME Bazaar:

1. The Fabric Feel & Breathability (Kapde Ki Quality)

Delhi summers and monsoon humidity demand breathable textiles. Pick pure cotton, linen blends, or high-grade cotton-poly weaves. Perform a quick texture check by feeling the soft finish against your skin.

2. The Shoulder Seam & Armhole Alignment

The shoulder seam of your casual shirt should sit right at the edge of your shoulder bone. If it sags down your arm, the shirt is too large; if it pulls tight across your back when you move, it is too small.

3. Seam and Button Durability Check

Inspect the double-stitching along the side seams and ensure buttons are securely cross-stitched. Good stitching prevents thread popping after just a few washes.

3. Wrinkle and Flexibility Check

Gently crush a section of the fabric in your palm for five seconds. A quality daily-wear cotton shirt will bounce back smoothly without harsh, deep creases.

5. Washability and Color Retention

Ensure the dyes used are colorfast. Premium retail choices at local Delhi stores use reactive dyes that preserve vibrant colors even after repeated hand or machine washing.
