Monsoon Garment Care Tips for Delhi Parents

  • Avoid Heavy Denim: Thick denim jeans trap moisture and take days to dry in monsoon air. Switch to light cotton joggers instead.
  • Dry in Well-Ventilated Indoor Spaces: Hang damp clothes directly under ceiling fans with adequate spacing between hangers.
  • Use Natural Antiseptics: Add a small cap of mild disinfectant liquid during rinse cycles to keep clothes smelling fresh and bacteria-free.
