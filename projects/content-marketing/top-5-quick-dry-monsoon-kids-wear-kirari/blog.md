Micro-Polyester Mesh</h3><p>Micro-polyester is naturally hydrophobic, meaning it repels water rather than absorbing it. Micro-mesh knits allow excellent airflow, preventing sweat build-up during humid rainy afternoons. It is perfect for active boys and girls who love outdoor sports.</p><h3>3. Nylon-Blend Activewear Fabric</h3><p>Nylon blends are extremely lightweight, tear-resistant, and dry exceptionally fast. Rayon and Viscose Fabrics</h3><p>Rayon is a semi-synthetic fiber made from natural cellulose. It offers a silky texture, drapes gracefully, and dries significantly faster than heavy woven fabrics. It is an ideal choice for stylish girls' wear, festive Kurtis, and lightweight printed shirts.</p><h3>5. Linen-Cotton Blends</h3><p>Linen offers superior breathability and quick-drying properties.
